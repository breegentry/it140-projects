# Project One Storyboard | Text-Based Adventure Game


## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

"Don't Upset the Witch" is a text-based game about an encounter with a witch gone wrong, forcing a transformed player to turn back into a human and escape the cottage of the witch.

**Storyline:**

After a bad encounter with the local witch, the player is cursed by the witch and must escape her cottage before she returns.  Weakened and transformed into a frog,
the player must gather items to break the curse and distract the witch's absurdly large cat who guards the exit.  To break the curse, 
the player must gather a magic wand and potion.  To distract the cat, players must find a fresh fish, a ball of yarn, and some catnip.  Finally, to escape the hut,
the player must find the key to the exit.  If the player fails to transform, is unable to distract the cat, or is unable to leave, this giant cat will surely 
eat them well before the witch would even return.

1. Ball of Yarn - Parlor

4. Fresh Fish - Kitchen
5. Potion of Strength - Hearth
6. Key - Closet


## Villain

The game's villian is the witch's giant cat.  Magically enhanced by the witch, the cat is large enough to eat the player whole, transformed or not.
However, like any cat, it can be easily distracted with toys, food, and catnip.
